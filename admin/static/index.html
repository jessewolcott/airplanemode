Advanced: use another device's MAC address

Some hotels tie the login to one device or limit devices per room. Signing in once on your phone, then entering your phone's Wi-Fi MAC address here, makes the box look like that phone to the hotel.

    Set up a WireGuard tunnel

    Paste the WireGuard config from your home router, your server (wg-easy, PiVPN…) or a VPN service. It starts with [Interface]. Make one config just for this box. The private key stays on the box and is never shown again.
